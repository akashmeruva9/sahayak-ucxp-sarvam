What it does — and doesn't — do yet

  • ✅ Live-verified with Sarvam: voice in/out, multilingual, multi-turn (ask ID → resolve), memory, confirmation before destructive actions.
  • ⚠️ Speech isn't perfect on IDs, so we confirm before cancelling — safer, one extra step.
  • ⚠️ In-app it's turn-based; true mid-sentence barge-in works on the phone-call path.
  • ⚠️ Single-step jobs today; multi-step workflows are on the roadmap. A working reference implementation — full deploy next.
